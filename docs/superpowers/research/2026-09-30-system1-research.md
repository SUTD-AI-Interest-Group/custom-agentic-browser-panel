# System-One decision models for a browser agent fast path — research map (2026-09-30)

Legend: **[M]** = number reported by the source; **[I]** = my inference. Our baseline for comparison: Von 1.3 = ModernBERT-large (395M), one forward pass, options packed with `[MASK]` markers, listwise softmax-CE + Brier, order-invariant since 1.2, temperature map `T = bias + w_H·H + w_len·log10(tokens)/4 + w_K·K/8`; JevBench sealed Choice hard 11.3, p50 0.096 s on 4 vCPU / 0.023 s on A10G [M] ([wfzyx/von](https://huggingface.co/wfzyx/von)).

## 1. Landscape

**Non-autoregressive scorers as the fast path.** The commercial reference is TypeSafe Jev ("70–500 ms typed decision" [M, vendor]); [jev-ultrafast](https://github.com/browser-use/jev-ultrafast) feeds it `[idx] role  name · value` rows of *visible* controls, asks operation + three speculative target heads in one round trip, lets a small LLM write text only for TYPE_TEXT, and reports Google Flights in 7.07 s / 101 protocol calls (down from 1,092) [M]. Encoder cross-encoders as *element rankers* are older and well measured: MindAct's DeBERTa-base (86M) reaches Recall@50 88.9/85.3/85.7 (cross-task/site/domain) with **random** negatives ([Mind2Web](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.06070v3)) [M]; DeBERTa-v3 184–434M rerankers over ALFWorld/WebShop/ScienceWorld recover 93% of full-data performance from 9.2% of target data, with "data diversity the primary driver, not capacity" ([Cross-Environment Neural Reranking, 2606.02204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02204)) [M]; a DeBERTa filter lifts LLaMA-2-70B on WebArena 1.97→2.96% ([2609.27770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27770)) [M]. No ≤400M model has *policy-level* results on WebArena/OSWorld/AndroidWorld; sub-1B encoders appear only inside pipelines [I, from absence].

**Dual-process agents with a learned fast path.** [CogniWeb](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.05081) is the closest architecture to ours: System 1 = SFT Phi-3-mini (3.8B) or Gemma-3-1b acting as a cross-/bi-encoder element scorer trained on MiniWoB++ + Mind2Web with a contrastive objective; System 2 = GPT-4o CoT; gate = learned sigmoid λ_t over state/goal/history plus rule triggers (3 repeated failures, invalid state). WebArena: 43.96% at 394 tokens/trajectory vs System-2-only 46.06% at 1,504 tokens and System-1-only **15.64%** [M] — the fast path alone is weak; the pairing is what pays. [DualSpec](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.07416) drafts with a no-reasoning LLM and accepts when a Yes/No critic's log-odds ≥ τ, τ tuned to ~20–30% intervention; up to 3.28× speed-up at parity, and *action-type matters*: Search decisions need reasoning, Visit decisions don't [M]. Speculative-execution variants: [Speculative Actions](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04371) (55% top-3 next-action in chess, 22–38% e-commerce, ~20% latency) [M]; [Dynamic Speculative Agent Planning, ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01920) (online-RL-adapted speculation, −30% cost) [M]; [AOSpec](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00881) (11.8–32.5% latency on Terminal-Bench) [M]; [Speculate with Memory](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12236) (+19–39% action-prediction accuracy from a transition table) [M]; [Second Thought](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13667) (reason in parallel while acting, up to −43% main-thread decoding) [M].

**Value/Q heads on frozen features.** [Digi-Q](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15760): MLP Q-head on frozen intermediate VLM features (after a short representation fine-tune), offline TD, best-of-N policy extraction; +21.2% over prior best on AitW without interaction [M]. [VEM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18906) trains an environment-free state-action value model [M]. RL for small *generative* policies works: [WebAgent-R1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16421) M-GRPO takes Qwen2.5-3B 6.1→33.9% and Llama-3.1-8B 8.5→44.8% on WebArena-Lite [M]; [UI-Voyager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24533) 4B reports 81.0% AndroidWorld; MAI-UI-2B 49.1% [M].

**Process reward models as step verifiers.** [Web-Shepherd](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15277) (Qwen2.5-3B / Qwen3-8B, LoRA, a11y tree + generated checklist, next-token loss over 40K preference pairs): WebRewardBench MRR 87.6/88.3 vs GPT-4o 67.4; as a verifier for a GPT-4o-mini policy on WebArena-lite 23.64→34.55% (GPT-4o-mini as verifier: 24.24%), ~10× cheaper; removing the checklist causes a "substantial drop" [M]. [GUI-Shepherd](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23738): 52k scored interactions; AndroidWorld +7.7 (RL reward) / +5.1 (verifier) [M]. [AgentPRM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08325): 0.5–8B backbones, 8× compute-efficient [M]. [Universal Verifier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06240): rubric + split process/outcome rewards drives false positives to near zero vs WebVoyager ≥45% [M].

**World-model verification.** Generative simulators (WMA, WebDreamer, ICLR 2025) are being replaced by *discriminative* ones: [Discriminative World Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02885) train Qwen3-8B with GRPO on 30,920 pairwise next-state-matching examples; state matching 80.8% vs WebWorld-8B 70.17 / WebDreamer-7B 74.51; WebArena-Lite best-of-5 28.48% vs 21.82 (no state) vs ReAct 13.94; 91.6 tokens/state vs 412.7 [M]. [WAC](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15384) (judge threshold 0.4, ≤3 correction rounds): VisualWebArena 24.5 vs 22.7, Online-Mind2Web 16.0 vs 14.7 [M] — small gains for a lot of latency [I]. Energy-based formulations: none with web/GUI results found.

## 2. Training-side methods

- **Hard negatives beat random.** [WEPO](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.10742) samples dispreferred elements by DOM distance to the lowest common ancestor and applies DPO: Llama-3-8B Mind2Web step SR 55.1→63.5, op-F1 65.8→76.1 vs SFT [M]. Mind2Web's own ranker used random negatives [M]. Retrieval work in 2025–26 converges on the same lesson ([Beyond Hard Negatives: score distribution matters](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04734)).
- **Soft-label distillation from an LLM improves calibration.** Converting an LLM's label + verbalized confidence into a soft target for a BERT student cut ECE 43.2% and Brier 34.0% averaged over datasets ([2605.11954](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11954)) [M]; teacher calibration itself is a KD factor ([2508.20224](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20224)) [M].
- **Distilling reasoning into a classifier.** Reasoning-LLM → 6-layer BERT with CE + teacher-CE + InfoNCE alignment retains 98.6% of teacher macro-F1 (0.7076 vs 0.6945 plain BERT) ([2510.11056](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.11056v3)) [M]. [DHRD](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21487): train-time-only rationale head + pooled class head, +0.65–5.47% relative, 96–142× QPS over CoT — measured on 3–8B decoders, not encoders [M]; transfer to an option-marker head is plausible but untested [I]. [LRKD](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.21611) does the same for cross-encoders. Reasoning-distilled *retrievers* exist at 150M ([Reason-ModernColBERT](https://huggingface.co/lightonai/Reason-ModernColBERT), beats ≤7B on BRIGHT) and 32M ([SmallReason-ColBERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29652), within 1.2 nDCG) [M].
- **Calibration losses.** Focal/dual-focal and Brier-weighted losses remain the standard ([Mukhoti et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/aeb7b30ef1d024a76f21a1d40e30c302-Paper.pdf)); Von already trains with Brier. Global temperature scaling degrades under shift ([CalAttn](https://openreview.net/forum?id=16dklduerp)); Von's own map drops 77.4 in-sample → 67.6 split-half on JevBench [M] — a refit on *our* distribution is mandatory before the gate uses it.
- **Order invariance.** Decoders show enormous position effects (72.1% vs 4.3% by slot under sandbagging, [2604.26206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26206)); fixes are content-free-placeholder calibration ([CapCal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10150), +10 NDCG at 0.6B) and permutation-aware GRPO ([PA-GRPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21016)) [M]. Von 1.2's marker masking gave zero flips over 4 orderings [M] — keep it; any new head must be re-checked the same way.
- **Auxiliary heads.** Evidence is indirect: Web-Shepherd's checklist (sub-goal progress) is load-bearing [M]; a fixed DONE token "installs reliably" (0.97±0.06) while open-ended corrections don't ([2607.17136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17136)) [M]; auxiliary future-action prediction gives +2.2% SR in visual planning ([2507.15130](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.15130)) [M]. No ablation of a "committing?" head on an action policy exists [I].
- **Curriculum / RL for small policies.** WebRL's failure-set curriculum and MobileRL's AdaGRPO difficulty weighting are the measured recipes ([survey 2604.27955](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.27955)); GRPO on scorers exists for rerankers ([ERank](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.00520), [ProRank](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.03487)) and the 8B discriminative WM. No GRPO on a ≤400M encoder found; no page-density curriculum found [I].

## 3. Representation

What the measured small models feed: MindAct = tag + text + salient attributes + **parent and child** text [M]; jev-ultrafast = visible-only `[idx] role name · value` [M]; Web-Shepherd = a11y tree + checklist [M]; [AgentOccam](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13825) keeps pivotal nodes plus ancestors/siblings/descendants [M]. Reduction ablations (all on LLM policies): compact a11y trees help weak models, full HTML helps strong ones, and diff-based history is the token-efficient way to carry outcomes ([Read More, Think More](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01535)) [M]; FocusAgent trims 50–80% at equal performance, LineRetriever 61–73% ([2507.00210](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.00210)) [M]. Vision/set-of-marks helps LLM agents (WALT/SkillWeaver ablations) but there is no text-only-encoder ablation of bbox/layout tokens [I]. For a 400M encoder the "weak model" regime applies: ranked, capped, neighbour-annotated controls with outcome history, not raw trees [I].

## 4. Escalation and gating

- [Conformal Arbitrage, NeurIPS 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.00911v1): gate on the **score gap** Δ = p_top − p_second, threshold set by conformal risk control on n≈400 labelled examples; MMLU at α=0.10 recovers 91% of the strong model's accuracy at 61% of its cost with 55.8% escalation [M].
- [LEC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01556): naive conformal thresholds don't bound the error *among accepted* predictions; constrain the selection-conditioned risk instead, extends to two-model routing [M].
- [UCCI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18796): isotonic regression on margin → calibrated error probability → cost-constrained threshold; 31% cost cut, ECE 0.12→0.03, beats entropy, split-conformal and FrugalGPT thresholds [M].
- [Bayesian self-escalation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24087): a learned competence posterior with a time-varying threshold beats post-hoc routing at equal cost; regret ∝ 1/√n calibration trajectories [M].
- Practice: DualSpec tunes τ to a target intervention rate; CogniWeb adds rule triggers. Measure with selective risk–coverage curves (AURC) and post-selection error, not ECE alone [I].

## 5. Emerging in 2026

- **Ettin** ([ICLR 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.11412)): same recipe/config as ModernBERT, `model_type: modernbert`, MIT. Ettin-enc-400m MNLI 91.3 vs ModernBERT-large 90.8, GLUE 90.8 vs 90.4; 150m 89.2 vs 89.1 [M]. Encoders beat matched decoders on classification; a 400M encoder beats a 1B decoder on MNLI, and continued pre-training doesn't close it [M]. The only *drop-in* backbone for our ORT-WebGPU export [I].
- **NeoBERT** (250M, ~RoBERTa-large GLUE), **mmBERT** (ModernBERT design, multilingual), **DeBERTaV3** (still wins a controlled matched-data comparison on NLU, [2504.08716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08716)) [M] — different architectures/ONNX paths, no long-context or WebGPU story [I].
- **Generative rerankers** (Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B, yes/no logit) top zero-shot classification at 8B ([BTZSC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11991)) [M] but are decoders — weaker per Ettin at ≤400M [I].
- **Hybrid encoder–decoder**: T5Gemma 2 ships 270M-270M ([2512.14856](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.14856)); no decision-model results [I].
- **Diffusion action models**: LLaDA-V 8B on Mind2Web; LLaDA-UI 16.7B MoE 8.95× faster than Qwen3-VL-8B ([2609.13287](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13287)) [M] — all ≥8B, not a ≤400M option [I].

## Ranked ideas for our fine-tune

1. **DOM-distance / sibling hard negatives + listwise loss over the real candidate space** (data, loss). Evidence: WEPO +8.4 SSR on Mind2Web; Mind2Web ranker trained on random negatives. Our candidate space already yields LCA-distance and same-role distractors. Expected: largest grounding gain per hour. Cost: low (data pipeline only). **Try first.**
2. **Replace the fixed 0.6/0.4 bands with a conformal/LEC-calibrated score-gap gate** (gating). Evidence: Conformal Arbitrage (n≈400), LEC, UCCI. Expected: a stated error bound among executed steps and higher accept rate at equal risk; zero retraining. Cost: low (~400 labelled Act steps from the eval). **Try first** — it changes the latency/accuracy trade-off without touching weights and pairs with the eval's kill criterion.
3. **Soft-label distillation from the chat LLM with verbalized confidence** (data/loss). Evidence: ECE −43%, Brier −34%. Expected: better-calibrated head, especially on the escalation margin. Cost: medium (one LLM pass over the corpus, teacher must be calibrated).
4. **Done/Blocked and "committing?" auxiliary heads with a sub-goal checklist in state** (heads/representation). Evidence: checklist ablation, reliable DONE token. Expected: fewer futile WAITs, safer point-of-no-return handling. Cost: medium.
5. **Swap backbone to Ettin-encoder-400m** (backbone). Evidence: +0.5 MNLI, +0.4 GLUE, drop-in. Expected: small, cheap. Cost: one retrain.
6. **Offline TD Q-head on frozen features from Act-loop logs (`success/no_change/stale`)** (self-training). Evidence: Digi-Q. Expected: environment-grounded values without RL infrastructure. Cost: medium-high (logging + head training).
7. **Train-time-only rationale head (DHRD/CRSD)** (heads). Evidence: +0.65–5.5% relative on decoders; 98.6% teacher retention for BERT. Expected: small; risk of no transfer to marker heads. Cost: medium (teacher rationales).
8. **Representation: neighbour context + diff-based outcome history + cross-site diversity over volume** (representation/data). Evidence: MindAct parent/child text, Read-More-Think-More, cross-env reranking. Expected: moderate, hard to attribute. Cost: low-medium.
