# Von internals and training recipe, read for our Act head

Sources. `V/` = https://github.com/wfzyx/von/blob/ca5d608fa79820d4e5388c9cb4c6a9b47a44a98c/ (the pin), `M/` = master on 2026-09-30, `H/` = https://huggingface.co/wfzyx/von/blob/5df8185a4f2327ad0a7cd117cc4f701ac557b9ae/, `R/` = our repo. "Measured" means the authors or our eval recorded it; "inferred" means my reading.

## 1. Architecture (from source)

- **Packing** `V/src/von/models/option_marker.py#L216-L228`: `"{question} {state}" [SEP] [MASK] opt0 [MASK] opt1 …`, wrapped in [CLS]/[SEP] by the tokenizer. Markers are found by `input_ids == mask_token_id` (`V/src/von/backends/option_marker_backend.py#L480-L482`), so a literal `[MASK]` in page text forges an option; 1.3.5 breaks it with a zero-width joiner (`M/src/von/models/option_marker.py#L160-L166`), our `neutralize` lowercases it. `split_digits` (`#L25-L39`) spaces every digit run; it is off in the shipped 1.2 weights (`H/marker_calibration.json` carries no `digit_split`).
- **Backbone** (`H/config.json`): ModernBERT-large, 28 layers, d = 1024, 16 heads of 64, GeGLU intermediate 2624, global attention on layers 0,3,…,27 (10 of 28), `local_attention: 128` = ±64 (`slidingWindow: 64` in our `von.json`), RoPE θ 160k global / 10k local, 8192 positions. The config still lists tasksource/ModernBERT-large-nli's 129 tasks, which is the lineage.
- **Head** `#L41-L60`: LayerNorm(1024) → Linear 1024→512 → GELU → LayerNorm(512) → Dropout 0.1 → Linear 512→1 on each marker's final hidden state; softmax across markers.
- **Independent-option masks** `#L63-L121`: each token gets an `option_id`, −1 for [CLS], question, state, separator and the trailing [SEP], k for marker k up to the token before marker k+1. `allowed = prefix→prefix | option→(prefix ∪ own span)` plus the diagonal. The sliding mask is `allowed & |p_i − p_j| ≤ 64` over the **reset** position ids, not raw index (`#L80-L86`). Two consequences (inferred): prefix hidden states never depend on any option, so they are cacheable; and in the 18 local layers an option token sees only the last ~64 prefix positions.
- **Position ids** `#L124-L156`: option k restarts at `prefix_len` (index of the first marker); the trailing [SEP] keeps its raw index. Invariance test: `V/tests/test_option_invariance.py#L50-L78`.
- **Forward** `#L180-L214`: one encoder pass, gather marker rows, score. OpenVINO traces the same graph with two additive float 4D masks plus `position_ids`, dynamic in batch and length (`backend.py#L124-L151`, `#L248-L285`).
- **Calibration map** `backend.py#L331-L374`: `T = clamp(bias + w_H·H/ln K + w_len·log10(state_tokens)/4 + w_K·K/8, lo, hi)`, shipped `bias 2.0151, entropy −3.2369, log_tokens 11.4916, n_options −3.5574, lo 0.3, hi 12` (`H/marker_calibration.json`), fitted on the 231 public JevBench items where K ≤ ~8. Inferred: at K ≥ ~12 the K term alone is below −5, so T sits at the 0.3 floor for every page we score; our fine-tune already replaced it with scalar T = 1.389 (`R/scripts/von-finetune/train.py#L279-L281`). Reported confidence is `(K·p_max − 1)/(K − 1)` (`#L34-L48`), not the README's top1−top2.
- **Noul prior** `#L543-L566`: a second, empty-state pass; `logit_yes −= a·(null_yes − null_no) + b`, shipped a = −0.5, b = 0.3, fallback 0.7·bias. Choice never uses it.

## 2. Training recipe

- **Loss** `V/training/train_option_marker.py#L275-L318`: listwise CE + 0.5·Brier over the marker softmax, both against a distribution, so a `target` list of soft labels is a drop-in (`#L237-L245`). "RLCD" (`V/training/train_rlcd.py#L83-L122`, README L142-151) is exactly this CE + λ·Brier, named after DeepMost's arXiv 2503.23303; there is no RL anywhere. Von 1.0 was a cross-encoder scored on the NLI entailment logit with one pass per option (`train_rlcd.py#L41-L80`).
- **Optimiser** `#L567-L579`: AdamW, wd 0.01, encoder at 0.5× and scorer at 2.5× of `--lr` (default 3e-5), 8 % warm-up, cosine, clip 1.0, fp16 AMP, batch 8 × accum 2 × 4 A10G. Length-bucketed batching under a token budget `min(8192, max(2048, GB·220))`, with 30 % of batches drawn from rows ≥ 2048 tokens (`#L99-L217`, `#L493-L531`). Continue-training: `--init_checkpoint`, lr 1e-5, 1 epoch, `--max_steps 6000` (`V/training/launch_continue_training.py#L129-L131`).
- **Shipped 1.2 recipe** (`H/marker_calibration.json`, key `training`): full retrain from the 1.1 weights under the new mask, 1 epoch, lr 3e-5; corpus "290k universal + 40k long-context + 40k synthetic two-hop/numeric, overlap_target 0.32". Universal = 49 generator tasks plus ANLI/WANLI, Banking77, legalbench, PAWS (`V/training/prepare_universal_dataset.py#L1-L11`); the assembled corpus was never uploaded (`V/training/build_continue_train.py#L7-L14`).
- **Shortcut hardening** (measured): the gold option was the highest-lexical-overlap option in 79.8 % of the corpus versus 56.4 % on JevBench hard, and Von picked top-overlap 86.8 % of the time. The fix subsamples shortcut-heavy sources to ~32 % gold-is-top, never 0 %, which "teaches the inverse shortcut" (`V/training/balance_corpus.py#L1-L25`, `harden_corpus.py#L1-L28`).
- **Distillation** `V/training/prepare_distill_dataset.py#L1-L27`: SargeDev/jev-distill-corpus-v3, soft targets from Jev 1.13 (yuri_v3, 444k rows; yuri_v1 dropped because every target is [0.5, 0.5]). It is absent from the shipped corpus line, so inferred unused in 1.2.
- **No permutation augmentation and no curriculum** in either trainer. 1.1's order sensitivity was measured (`V/benchmarks/permutation_ensemble.py`, `analyze_permutations.py`: 49.5 % of hard-tier answers flipped across 4 orderings) and removed architecturally in 1.2.
- **Benchmarks**: jabr v2 (49 tasks, 869 cases, out-of-domain routing; Von 72.0 % macro), JevBench public easy 48 / standard 72 / hard 111 plus sealed halves (argmax scoring; Calibration = ½ ECE half + ½ TVD half, `V/benchmarks/fit_calibration.py#L1-L20`), Decision Index (2–255 options, refuse rather than truncate, `di_engine.py`), ViZDoom (`doom_eval.py#L142-L147`: three actions per scenario, not the README's "six"; state = labelled sprite positions plus depth-buffer medians), `routing_auc.py` (can confidence route? hard-tier AUC 0.632 per `balance_corpus.py#L7-L8`).
- **What the probes taught them**: `probe_marker_distance#L3-L7`: the corpus's longest premise was 116 tokens, so every training example fit one local window and the global pathway was never exercised; the lookup probe still reads to ~2048 tokens, so the hard-tier gap is compositional (`H/README.md` L131-134). `compare_longctx`/`prepare_long_context_dataset#L7-L13`: accuracy fell from 40.7 % (≤ 512 tokens) to 23.5 % (2–4k) before the long-context corpus. `probe_overlap_bias`/`probe_paraphrase_adversary`: sealed paraphrase_robustness 7 % against 29 % chance; PAWS twins were added. `probe_numeral_sensitivity`: 70 % of picks survive scrambling every digit, hence `digit_split` and contrastive numeric twins (`generate_numeric_decisions.py#L15-L21`). `probe_noul_negation`: 24 % flips under rubric negation and a 22 pp yes-bias, hence 20 % neg-wrap augmentation. `probe_native_logit` (the pinned commit): Qwen3.5-4B read out on its own letter logits scores 94.4 % on the public standard tier against Von's 63.9 %, the "Von 2 feasibility" question. `eval_hard_fast#L8-L16`: 4 processes × 1 thread beat 1 × 4 threads by 2× on CPU (877 ms vs 468 ms per hard item). `stat_gate#L3-L7`: gates on n = 12–15 have a minimum detectable effect wider than a 20 pp difference; every claim now passes paired McNemar plus MDE. That applies to us: 50/54 vs 52/54 grounding is unresolvable.

## 4. Where our 400–800 ms goes, and what can change without a from-scratch retrain

Measured on our fixtures (`R/scripts/system1-eval/report.json`): the act pass takes 225–300 ms at 6 options, 380 ms at 15–20, 817 ms at 47–49; the value head 225–308 ms; golden cases T = 70 → 84 ms, T = 263–297 → 170–190 ms. Inferred fit: **≈ 80 ms fixed + ≈ 0.65 ms per token**, with T ≈ 150 + 20·K.

FLOPs per token from the config: linear 24.5 MFLOP per layer → 686 MFLOP; dense attention 4·T·d per layer → 126 MFLOP at T = 1100, 264 at T = 2300. That is ≈ 1.2 TFLOP/s effective on a GPU whose fp32 peak exceeds 10 TFLOP/s, so **kernel efficiency, not the architecture, is the cost**. Von's mask only needs each option to see P + L ≈ 170 keys (0.7 MFLOP per layer per token), but ORT multiplies the full T×T product and adds the mask, so the sparsity buys nothing today.

| mechanism | what the code allows today | expected effect |
|---|---|---|
| Prefix once, options in chunks | Exact by construction (prefix never attends to options). The exported graph has no K/V inputs and HF ModernBERT has no cache path, so it needs a two-stream wrapper in `export.py` | The prefix is ~150 of ~1000 tokens and changes every step (scroll, recent actions): ≤ 5 % within a pass, 0 across steps. Chunking re-pays the prefix per chunk and only trims the attention term (10–25 % at K = 47–120) minus padding. Low value. |
| fp16 activations (`shader-f16`) | The eval env reports `shaderF16: true`; an fp16 graph passed parity except that converters put RoPE `pos·inv_freq` in fp16 (`R/scripts/export-von/export.py#L283-L284`) | Apple GPUs run fp16 at twice the fp32 rate and half the bandwidth: 1.4–2× on the per-token term. Keep RoPE, LayerNorm and softmax in fp32. |
| CPU-partitioned nodes | 540 of 1909 nodes (Gather 201, Unsqueeze 122, Div 84, plus 15 Memcpys) run on ORT's implicit CPU EP: int64 shape, RoPE and mask subgraphs (`R/scripts/system1-eval/README.md` L548-555) | Three `Div` per layer means a GPU↔CPU sync inside every layer. Feeding cos/sin tables and int32 mask indices as inputs removes them and unlocks graph capture with bucketed padding (a pad token with `option_id −2` is already handled by the mask formula). Plausibly most of the ~80 ms fixed cost. |
| Fewer layers | Trainer and exporter are layer-count agnostic (`build_q8` asserts 3·n_layers) | Latency scales with layers: 28→22 saves ~21 % and drops 2 of the 10 global layers. Needs the continue-train to recover; the authors listed depth pruning as a step and skipped it only because they cleared the speed line. |
| Early exit | Not expressible in the static graph; needs per-depth scorer heads | Skip. |
| Local-attention window | `sliding_window` is read from config at trace time; shrinking it changes the trained function | No latency gain, the kernel is dense anyway. The window does mean 18 of 28 layers see only the last 64 prefix tokens from an option, so put Goal and Provided values at the prefix tail. |
| Marker and option tokens | The text is ours | Each option costs ~20 tokens × 0.65 ms. Dropping the `[id]`, role word and arrow saves 4–6 tokens (≈ 100–160 ms at K = 47); the marker index already identifies the option. |
| Second passes | `worker.ts#L133` scores questions serially | Von's mask makes marker groups mutually blind, so value and blocked groups can ride the same pass with a per-group softmax: −225…350 ms on every field or blocked step. Retrain required. |

## 5. "System One" in Von's writing

Von defines System One as "reflexive, parallel, deterministic, and statistically calibrated decision-making" in "a single forward pass without autoregressive text generation" (`V/README.md` L56-58), the encoder acting as an "in-context semantic verifier" of state against described criteria (L410). The Choice / Noul / Score primitives follow TypeSafe's `/v1/systemone` protocol (Jev 1.13, proprietary). Systems it measures against: Laya (ModernBERT-large), jeff (GLiFormer), GLiNER2, Verdict 1.4 (151M), the kev-* pointer heads on decoders (25–36 composite), hopper (Qwen3.5-4B LoRA native-logit readout, 59.4) (`M/README.md` L119-126, `M/hf/MODEL_CARD.md` L130-133). Cited methods: DeepMost RLCD (arXiv 2503.23303) and ModernBERT; benchmarks jabr, JevBench, Decision Index, morethanamachine's ViZDoom protocol. Two usage rules they state: bare labels fail, give descriptive criteria (README L408-423); Noul without criteria is the weakest path.

## Ranked modifications for our task

1. **fp16 activations with fp32 RoPE/norm islands.** 1.4–2× on the token term (400 → ~250 ms, 817 → ~500 ms). Evidence: shader-f16 present; the earlier fp16 graph failed only on RoPE precision. Cost ≈ 1 day: export flag, parity gate, WebGPU parity in the eval.
2. **Remove the CPU-partitioned subgraphs**, then bucketed padding plus `enableGraphCapture`. Targets the ~80 ms fixed cost and the per-layer syncs. Profile with ORT first. Cost 1–2 days; speculative until profiled.
3. **Fan-out pack**: value and blocked_reason groups inside the act pass with a per-group softmax. Exact under Von's mask; −225…350 ms on field and blocked steps. Cost ≈ 1 day across the worker protocol, `render.mjs` and `train.py`; rides the planned Mind2Web fine-tune.
4. **Leaner rendering and prefix reorder** (goal and values last, shorter option lines). 15–25 % fewer tokens on dense pages and every layer sees the goal. Cost: hours plus the scheduled fine-tune; gate by McNemar on the 3.5k-row val, not the 54 cases.
5. **Depth pruning to 22 layers plus continue-train.** ≈ −20 % latency; accuracy risk, so ship as an A/B checkpoint. Cost ≈ 1 day plus eval.
6. **K-aware calibration refit** on our val (entropy, K, state tokens; k-fold scalar vs map as in `von calibrate`). Restores gate coverage at K ≈ 47, where tops are 0.36–0.50 today, without moving the argmax. Cost: hours.

Longer term: a 149M ModernBERT-base option-marker model would be 2.5–3× faster but must be trained from scratch (Von's corpus builders are public, the assembled corpus is not), days plus GPU spend. A prefix-shared block-attention kernel is worth ≤ 25 % at K ≥ 47 and 2–3 days.
